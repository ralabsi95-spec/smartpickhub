## Editorial page pattern
Every guide should answer the purchase decision quickly:
1. Who should buy / who should not buy.
2. A concise comparison table with exact model identifiers and region-specific differences.
3. Decision criteria and compatibility checks.
4. Manufacturer-source links adjacent to the specifications they support.
5. Explicit limitations, trade-offs and regional variants.
6. Affiliate disclosure beside links and in the site disclosure page.
7. No invented prices, ratings, testing claims or direct ASIN URLs.

## Search-intent rules
- Target one primary intent per guide and use related terms naturally in headings and copy.
- Do not create separate pages for near-identical phrase variants.
- Do not force the word “best” or “2026” into a title unless the page genuinely supports a current best-of claim and can be maintained.
- Use UK spelling and UK product model/voltage/plug details first; identify US counterparts separately.
- Keep titles descriptive, unique and readable; write unique meta descriptions that explain the comparison and the buyer's decision.
- Add internal links between genuinely related guides, not sitewide keyword-stuffed links.
- Product model comparisons are high-intent opportunities, but must be verified against current regional manufacturer pages before targeting exact model-vs-model terms.

## Indexing and measurement
- Google: submit `https://smartpickhub.pages.dev/sitemap-index.xml` in Google Search Console. Use URL Inspection for a small number of priority URLs; do not repeatedly request the same URL.
- Bing and participating IndexNow engines: publish the IndexNow key file, then notify changed URLs. Check Bing Webmaster Tools for crawl and IndexNow status.
- IndexNow does not submit to Google. Google Search Console and sitemap are separate.
- Monitor Search Console by query/page/country/device, plus GA4 engaged sessions and affiliate outbound clicks.
- First 30-day review: impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, indexed vs excluded pages, top query/page pairs, Amazon outbound clicks. Do not call a keyword a winner until actual query and conversion evidence exists.
- Do not buy links, generate doorway pages, or mass-publish thin keyword variations.

## Research observations (19 November 2026)
Live search results show active, current UK buyer-guide SERPs for air fryers, robot vacuums, cordless vacuums, espresso machines and dash cams. These results establish that the topics have active commercial search intent and strong editorial competition; they do **not** provide reliable monthly search-volume or keyword-difficulty numbers. The site should differentiate through exact UK/US model distinctions, practical compatibility checks, transparent manufacturer-source citations and useful trade-off analysis.
