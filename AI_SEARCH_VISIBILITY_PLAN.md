# SmartPickHub — AI Search Visibility Plan

**Status:** Research and implementation plan; documentation only.  
**Date:** 19 November 2026  
**Primary market:** United Kingdom  
**Secondary market:** United States  
**Production:** https://smartpickhub.pages.dev/  
**Repository:** https://github.com/ralabsi95-spec/smartpickhub

## 1. Objective

Improve the likelihood that existing SmartPickHub buying guides can be discovered, understood, cited and linked by AI-assisted search experiences, while also improving conventional organic search and purchase-intent conversion.

Target surfaces:
- ChatGPT Search (OpenAI)
- Google Search AI Overviews and AI Mode, plus Gemini Apps grounding
- Microsoft Bing and Copilot
- Perplexity Search
- Claude Search and user-directed Claude retrieval

This plan does not promise inclusion, citations, rankings, traffic or sales. Eligibility and citation activity are controlled by each provider and are not guaranteed.

## 2. Verified current technical baseline

- Production robots response is generated by `src/pages/robots.txt.js`. For the production hostname `smartpickhub.pages.dev`, code emits `User-agent: *`, `Allow: /`, and the sitemap-index URL. Preview builds emit `User-agent: *` and `Disallow: /`.
- The production wildcard allow means named crawlers inherit permission unless a provider-specific rule or infrastructure control overrides it. In particular, it does not prove that a crawler has actually visited or successfully fetched the site.
- The preview remains intentionally excluded from crawling.
- `src/layouts/Layout.astro` emits canonical URLs, meta descriptions, index/follow on production, noindex/nofollow on preview, semantic navigation and a main content landmark.
- `src/pages/guides/[slug].astro` emits Article, BreadcrumbList and ItemList/Product JSON-LD, plus semantic article headings, a comparison table, a buyer checklist, product details, manufacturer source links, and clear paid-link disclosures.
- The guide body states that its product notes are research-led and not hands-on testing. Amazon marketplace search URLs are labelled as searches, not direct verified listings.
- The 25-guide / 100-product catalogue is the protected baseline. No new pages or product imports are required for this plan.

## 3. Provider-specific crawler and control distinctions

### OpenAI / ChatGPT
- `OAI-SearchBot` is the crawler relevant to ChatGPT Search discovery and citation eligibility.
- `GPTBot` is a separate control for content that may be used to train OpenAI foundation models.
- `ChatGPT-User` is associated with user-triggered retrieval, not automatic search crawling.
- OpenAI documents that ChatGPT referral URLs include `utm_source=chatgpt.com`, which can be measured in analytics.
- Current production wildcard allows all three by robots inheritance. Actual Cloudflare/WAF passage and visits are not verified.

### Google Search and Gemini
- Googlebot eligibility and indexing are foundational for AI Overviews and AI Mode; there is no special AI-only schema requirement.
- Google-Extended is a separate robots product token controlling use of crawled content for future Gemini model training and grounding in Gemini Apps / Vertex AI. It does not control Google Search inclusion and is not a Search ranking signal.
- The current production wildcard does not disallow Google-Extended, so the default permission applies.
- Google Search Central says `llms.txt`, forced content chunking, special GEO hacks and inauthentic mentions are not required for Google AI Search. Focus on useful, original, crawlable content and ordinary technical SEO.
- Search Console's generative-AI performance reporting should be inspected if available once the owner connects the property.

### Microsoft / Bing / Copilot
- Bingbot access and indexing remain prerequisites for Bing discovery.
- Bing Webmaster Tools provides an AI Performance report for cited pages, grounding queries and citation trends across Copilot and supported partner experiences. It is an AI citation metric, not a click, ranking or traffic metric; data is sampled/aggregated.
- Microsoft announced independent AI Training and AI Grounding preferences on 30 October 2026. Both default to Allow when no preference is specified. The new preferences begin being honored as Microsoft crawls from 1 February 2027. AI Grounding is page-level; AI Training has a robots.txt shared token and page-level control.
- No `NoArchive` or `NoCache` directive is present in the current site template. No Microsoft preference change is needed now. Recheck official guidance before February 2027.

### Perplexity
- `PerplexityBot` is the crawler for Perplexity search discovery and is distinct from `Perplexity-User`, which supports user-requested page retrieval.
- The production wildcard currently permits both by inheritance.
- Perplexity's official documentation advises allowing PerplexityBot and permitting its published IP ranges where a WAF blocks requests. Do not create Cloudflare WAF exceptions without first inspecting current zone rules and verifying official IP ranges.

### Anthropic / Claude
- `Claude-SearchBot` supports search discovery; `Claude-User` supports user-requested retrieval; `ClaudeBot` is the separate model-training crawler.
- The production wildcard currently permits all three by inheritance.
- Anthropic states its bots respect robots.txt. Its current documentation also describes a shared AI-training preference rollout; this area is evolving and should be rechecked before any policy edit.

## 4. Editorial improvements that help both people and AI retrieval

Apply these to existing guides only, prioritised by UK commercial intent:
1. Lead with a concise, accurate decision summary: which buyer need the guide serves, the main trade-off, and what the reader should verify before purchase.
2. Keep exact manufacturer model names, model variants and UK/US distinctions explicit. Never merge region-specific SKUs or voltage variants.
3. Make comparisons decision-led: capacity, compatibility, performance limits, ownership costs, consumables, subscriptions, warranty and installation constraints as relevant to the category.
4. Keep every material product claim tied to the official manufacturer/support source already stored in the catalogue. Distinguish manufacturer specifications from editorial interpretation.
5. State limitations and uncertainty plainly. Do not claim hands-on testing, current retailer stock, current prices, star ratings, review counts or direct Amazon product URLs without evidence.
6. Keep the existing semantic heading hierarchy, comparison table, buyer checklist, product sections, canonical URLs and structured data aligned with visible page content.
7. When a guide is substantively refreshed, store a real `updatedAt` date and use it for visible update metadata and Article `dateModified`; never advance a date without an actual content review.
8. Add FAQs only where they answer real buyer questions and improve the guide. Do not create thin question pages or add FAQ schema for the sake of AI visibility.
9. Add useful original analysis rather than paraphrasing competitor pages or generating many prompt-specific near-duplicates.
10. Maintain clear affiliate disclosure and explain whether each retailer link is a search query or a verified direct listing.

## 5. Measurement framework — free-first

Keep these measures separate:
- Google Search Console: indexed pages, impressions, organic clicks, CTR and queries; generative-AI report if available.
- Bing Webmaster Tools: indexed pages, search queries and AI Performance citations/grounding queries.
- GA4 (after owner supplies Measurement ID and agrees consent/privacy approach): organic sessions, AI referrals (including ChatGPT UTM), engagement and `amazon_outbound_click` events by guide/product/market/link type.
- Amazon Associates UK/US reports: clicks, ordered items and commissions. These are not interchangeable with onsite outbound-click events.
- Pinterest: feed connection status and actual Pins/analytics only when account evidence is available.

Manual AI answer sampling can later use a small fixed prompt set across ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity and Claude. Record date, exact prompt, whether SmartPickHub was cited, cited URL, competitor sources and whether the result showed a purchase/comparison intent. Do not treat one-off answer tests as stable rankings or buy paid visibility checks.

## 6. Safe implementation sequence

1. Confirm production deployment and crawler responses from Cloudflare Pages; distinguish robots permission from actual bot fetches.
2. Owner connects GSC, GA4 and Bing Webmaster Tools using their own accounts. Never request passwords or 2FA in chat.
3. Decide separately whether model-training use is permitted. Search/citation access and model-training access are not the same preference. Do not alter GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended or shared training preferences without an explicit owner decision.
4. Review one existing Tier-1 guide at a time against the editorial checklist; prepare changes in a branch/PR.
5. Validate build, JSON-LD, canonical, sitemap, robots preview/production separation, accessibility and Lighthouse on exact PR head.
6. Merge only after exact-head CI succeeds and required owner approval is present.
7. Measure outcomes over time; do not infer causal improvement from citation fluctuations alone.

## 7. Explicit non-goals

- No paid AI visibility tools, subscriptions, TinyFish credits or paid checks.
- No automatic publication, mass AI-generated pages, synthetic reviews, invented experience or fake third-party mentions.
- No speculative `llms.txt` implementation claimed to improve Google ranking.
- No broad Cloudflare WAF bypass or bot allowlisting without evidence and a verified security review.
- No catalogue expansion or new public guide solely to target an AI prompt.
- No crawler policy or DNS change without an explicit, informed owner decision.
