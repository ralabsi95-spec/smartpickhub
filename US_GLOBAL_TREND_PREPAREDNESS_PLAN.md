## 1. Strategic decision

Operate three complementary discovery lanes:

1. **Evergreen buyer demand:** durable product needs with commercial/transactional intent, researched independently for UK and US.
2. **Event-driven demand:** games, launches, major releases, seasonal shopping, weather events, outages and other verified developments. Capture the spike early, then monitor decay.
3. **Civil preparedness and resilience:** useful, non-alarmist household readiness for ordinary disruptions such as power, water, communications and severe weather. Cover official guidance and product utility, not war speculation.

A trend is not automatically a buying opportunity. A candidate advances only when there is a real product relationship, buyer intent, suitable catalogue fit, reliable evidence, regional availability and a differentiated page purpose.

## 4. UK and US official preparedness evidence

The official guidance reviewed is about household resilience to emergencies and temporary disruption. It is not a prediction of war or a statement that a conflict is imminent.

### United Kingdom — Be Ready

The UK Cabinet Office published updated Be Ready advice on 22 October 2026, encouraging households to prepare for three days of disruption. The official checklist includes:
- bottled water (3 litres per person per day for drinking; additional water may be needed for cooking and washing);
- non-perishable food;
- torch and spare batteries;
- FM radio powered by batteries or wind-up mechanism;
- charged power bank;
- blankets, sleeping bags and warm clothing;
- cash, copies of important documents and contact details;
- first-aid supplies, essential medicines, baby supplies and pet supplies as relevant.

Official sources:
- https://www.gov.uk/government/news/households-encouraged-to-take-small-simple-steps-to-stay-prepared-in-an-emergency
- https://beready.campaign.gov.uk/be-ready-for-emergencies/

### United States — FEMA / Ready.gov

FEMA's basic emergency-supply guidance includes:
- water and non-perishable food for several days;
- battery-powered or hand-crank radio, with NOAA weather-alert capability where appropriate, plus spare batteries;
- flashlight, first-aid kit and whistle;
- phone charger and backup battery;
- sanitation supplies, manual can opener, local maps and important documents;
- dust mask and shelter-in-place supplies where appropriate to local hazards.

US water guidance commonly states one US gallon per person per day for several days. Do not copy UK quantities or radio-band recommendations into US product pages without regional qualification.

Official source:
- https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_hm-emergency-supply-kit-checklist_english.pdf
- https://www.ready.gov/kit

### European Union

The European Commission's Preparedness Union Strategy includes a population-preparedness objective of minimum 72-hour self-sufficiency. It is an EU-level framework and should not be represented as an identical national shopping list for every member state.
- https://commission.europa.eu/topics/preparedness_en

## 6. Trend radar operating method

### Inputs

1. Google Trends Trending Now: US, UK and selected worldwide/country views.
2. Official announcements from manufacturers, game publishers, public agencies and standards bodies.
3. Search-result features and related queries for buyer-intent validation.
4. OpenSEO/DataForSEO keyword metrics when credits are available.
5. Reddit/community discussions as qualitative discovery only; corroborate claims with primary sources.
6. First-party GSC query/page data when the verified property is connected.

### Candidate scoring (100 points)

- Buyer intent and purchase proximity: 20
- Evidence that the trend is real and current: 15
- Relevant product adjacency: 15
- US/UK market fit and model availability: 15
- SERP opportunity and realistic competition: 10
- Trend persistence / evergreen potential: 10
- Catalogue and editorial fit: 10
- Compliant imagery and affiliate eligibility: 5

Unknown metrics remain unknown; do not award points by guessing. A viral search with no sensible product connection should be logged and discarded from commerce work.

### Trend lifecycle

DETECT → VERIFY SOURCE → CLASSIFY EVENT / SEASONAL / EVERGREEN → MAP BUYER NEED → CHECK US + UK SERPS → RESEARCH KEYWORD METRICS → CHECK PRODUCT IDENTITY / REGIONAL VARIANT → ESTIMATE CONTENT HALF-LIFE → DRAFT BRIEF → QA → OWNER APPROVAL → PUBLISH ONLY IF AUTHORISED → MONITOR / RETIRE

### Cadence proposal

- Daily: lightweight scan of Google Trends US/UK and official announcements for material event spikes.
- Weekly: consolidate candidates, deduplicate, map them to existing guides, and shortlist only the strongest buyer-intent opportunities.
- Monthly: review seasonality and decay; remove stale event opportunities from the active queue.
- Quarterly: revisit the evergreen category map and US/UK coverage gaps.

This is a proposed cadence only. No background job, scheduled automation, paid research or AI tracking has been enabled.
