**Include:** upright cordless wet-dry floor washers that vacuum liquid spills and wash sealed hard floors in one pass.

- Workshop/garage wet-dry shop vacuums.
- Carpet extractors and deep carpet cleaners.

## 3. Recommended article structure
1. **Short answer / comparison table** — model, intended floor type, cleaning mode, clean/dirty tank capacities, manufacturer-stated runtime, self-clean/dry method, weight, warranty and current US availability-check date.
2. **Best wet-dry floor cleaners by buyer need** — only after enough models pass product and availability verification. Possible labels: best steam option, best for pet hair, best low-profile reach, best value, best for large hard-floor areas. Do not assign awards without comparative evidence.
3. **What a wet-dry floor cleaner actually does** — suction plus powered roller and clean/dirty water separation; how it differs from a shop vac, steam mop, robot mop and stick-vac attachment.
4. **Hard-floor compatibility** — sealed hardwood, tile, vinyl and laminate restrictions; do not assume all models are safe on every wood finish.
5. **Steam and hot-water modes** — distinguish heater/internal temperature from actual outlet temperature; identify manufacturer test claims and floor-material restrictions.
6. **Pet hair and sticky spills** — roller anti-tangle design, hair removal, dirty-tank handling, odour prevention and post-clean maintenance.
7. **Battery runtime and tank capacity** — compare runtime by mode, charging time, clean/dirty tank sizes and whether published runtime includes steam/hot-water operation.
8. **Self-cleaning and drying docks** — what is rinsed, whether hot water/steam is used, drying temperature/time, noise and manual cleaning still required.
9. **Weight, steering and under-furniture reach** — distinguish physical weight from powered wheel assistance; record minimum lay-flat clearance.
10. **Ownership costs and consumables** — proprietary cleaning solution, replacement rollers/filters, battery replacement policy, warranty and service.
11. **How to choose** — hard-floor percentage, room size, pets, steam need, storage, weight and tolerance for after-use maintenance.
12. **FAQ** — can it clean carpets; can it pick up dry debris; can it run without water; is steam safe for sealed hardwood; how often should the dirty tank/roller be cleaned; are cleaning solutions interchangeable?

## 5. Comparison methodology — no false testing claims
SmartPickHub has not physically tested these machines. The page must say so plainly. Use a source-attributed comparison based on:
- Official manufacturer specifications and manuals.
- Independent hands-on testing from reputable reviewers, cited per model.
- Warranty/US support and replacement consumable availability.
- Current US product and retailer stock checks, dated at each update.
- Amazon.com product identity and affiliate destination verification only after matching the exact model.

Do not compare Pa/kPa claims as if they were independently standardized test scores. Record each mode's runtime separately; never imply maximum runtime applies to steam mode unless the source says so. Avoid presenting internal heater temperature as delivered steam temperature.
