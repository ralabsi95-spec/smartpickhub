**Purpose:** Identify where to spend the next evidence-led research effort for the 1,000-product roadmap without duplicating existing guides.

## 1. Method and limitations

This is a first-pass public search-result review, not a full keyword research batch. Search result composition is used to identify buyer tasks, competitor formats and likely evidence requirements. Search snippets may be stale or incomplete. No numerical demand or ranking-difficulty claims are made.

Before any candidate is promoted, the next research pass must capture:
- exact UK queries and close variants;
- dominant and secondary intent;
- SERP composition and the actual top result set;
- GSC query evidence when the property is connected, or a dated keyword-tool export when approved;
- product/model and official manufacturer evidence;
- current UK retailer destination checks;
- existing-guide overlap and a distinct editorial angle.

## 4. What the observed SERPs teach us

The better UK comparison pages do not simply list products. They help the shopper make category-specific decisions:
- Dehumidifiers: compressor vs desiccant, extraction rate, low-temperature performance, noise, tank size, laundry mode and running cost.
- Heated airers: form factor, usable drying space, wattage, drying time evidence, storage and cost per load.
- Power stations: usable watt-hours, continuous and surge output, weight, solar input, battery chemistry, charging time and UPS transfer behaviour.
- Air purifiers: CADR, room size, air changes, noise, filter availability and replacement cost.
- Heaters: room size, thermostat, safety protections, wattage and cost calculation.

Several competitor pages explicitly rely on hands-on testing. SmartPickHub must not imitate those claims. A research-led guide can still be useful if it:
1. clearly says it is manufacturer-source research, not hands-on testing;
2. provides accurate exact-model and compatibility comparisons;
3. shows calculations with assumptions and formulae;
4. identifies trade-offs and missing evidence;
5. explains who should not buy each option;
6. links to primary sources close to the claims.

Where the SERP is dominated by strong independent lab tests, do not pretend to replace those tests. Look for a narrower, defensible decision task or deprioritise the category.

## 5. First-pass priority order

This is a research sequencing decision, not a measured demand ranking:

1. **Dehumidifiers** — no existing guide; clear UK buyer tasks; substantial evidence burden and strong competition.
2. **Heated clothes airers** — no existing guide; seasonal UK relevance; useful comparison variables.
3. **Portable power station guide improvement** — existing URL; improve product depth and use-case matching before any new URL.
4. **Electric blankets / heated throws** — distinct seasonal product task; requires official safety and model evidence.
5. **UPS for router/NAS** — specific technical buyer need; verify commercial search intent before committing.
6. **Portable heaters** — potentially valuable but safety and running-cost claims require careful evidence.
7. **Window vacuums / steam cleaners** — lower-priority cleaning extensions; test distinct SERP intent and overlap first.

Do not convert this ordering into product counts or assume that every category deserves a published page.

## 6. Product sourcing rules for these categories

For each exact model:
- Start with manufacturer UK product/support pages and manuals.
- Record exact model number, variant and current lifecycle status.
- Extract only supported specifications; distinguish maximum, typical and mode-specific values.
- Check UK plug/voltage, warranty, accessories, replacement parts and regional availability.
- Verify Amazon UK destination separately. A manufacturer page does not establish Amazon stock; an Amazon search link is not a direct listing.
- Do not publish prices, discounts, ratings, review counts or “in stock” statements from a stale search snippet.
- Product images remain optional; no image is better than an unlicensed or mismatched image.
