## Evidence matrix
### Whynter ARC-1050XPH
Official Whynter US product page identifies ARC-1050XPH and SKU ARC-1050XPH; UPC 850014459642. The official manual distinguishes 13,500 BTU ASHRAE / 10,000 BTU SACC cooling and 10,500 BTU heating, 500 ft² coverage, 115V/60Hz. Keep ASHRAE and SACC ratings distinct; the 10,500 BTU heat figure is separate from cooling.

### Whynter ARC-102CS
Official Whynter US product page identifies ARC-102CS and links a current 2024 manual. Current product page/manual list 10,000 BTU ASHRAE / 7,000 BTU SACC, 300 ft² coverage, R32 refrigerant and 115V/60Hz. An older 2019 manual contains different capacity/refrigerant figures; do not mix those obsolete specifications with the current model page/manual.

### Rachio 3 Smart Sprinkler Controller
Official Rachio catalogue lists 8-zone SKU **8ZULW-CP** and 16-zone SKU **16ZULW-C**. Keep one Rachio 3 family candidate with zone-count variants; do not create two model records. Both variants showed in stock at catalogue capture, but this is manufacturer-store availability only.

### Rachio Smart Hose Timer
Official Rachio page identifies the 1 Valve + WiFi Hub kit, SKU **SHVK001**. The valve requires two AA batteries and the kit requires a WiFi Hub; the system communicates over 2.4GHz Wi-Fi via the hub. Official catalogue showed the kit out of stock at capture.

### Aqara Smart Lock U100
Official Aqara US product page and product specification sheet identify Smart Lock U100. Confirmed characteristics include Bluetooth 5.0, Zigbee 3.0 and NFC; US-standard fingerprint deadbolt; 35–55mm door thickness; IP65 keypad; four AA batteries. Exact manufacturer SKU/package code remains pending.

### Schlage Encode Plus Smart WiFi Deadbolt
Official Schlage Encode Plus page confirms the model family. Schlage's official electronics comparison chart maps the Encode Plus deadbolt to model **BE499**. Trim/finish choices are configurations, not distinct base models.

### Schlage Encode Smart WiFi Deadbolt
Official Schlage Encode page confirms the model family. The official Schlage comparison chart maps the Encode deadbolt to **BE489**; the Encode lever uses FE789 and is a different product form, not this candidate. Preserve the deadbolt/lever distinction.

### Greenworks GPW2003
Official Greenworks product/support page confirms model **GPW2003**, SKU **5118202**; 2000 PSI at 1.2 GPM, 14A, 120V, 25-ft hose, 35-ft power cord and 3-year tool warranty.

### Greenworks GPW2303A
Official Greenworks product/support page confirms model **GPW2303A**, SKU **5128502**; Gen 2 2300 PSI at 1.2 GPM (2.3 GPM at 100 PSI), brushless motor, five nozzles and 3-year tool / 10-year motor warranty.

### Greenworks GPW3001
Official Greenworks product/support page confirms model **GPW3001**, SKU **5113902**; 3000 PSI max, 1.1 GPM at 3000 PSI (2.0 GPM at 100 PSI), brushless motor and 3-year tool / 10-year motor warranty. Do not misstate 2.0 GPM as the flow rate at 3000 PSI.

### Kärcher K 4 Power Control
Official Kärcher US product page and US datasheet confirm order number **1.324-045.0**, 120V/60Hz, maximum 1900 PSI and 1.5 GPM. This is the US 120V version; do not substitute European 230V models.

### Kärcher K2300PS
Official Kärcher US product page and datasheet confirm order number **1.106-222.0**, 120V/60Hz, 2300 PSI and 1.2 GPM. Use manufacturer values; third-party retail snippets can show incompatible pressure/flow figures.
