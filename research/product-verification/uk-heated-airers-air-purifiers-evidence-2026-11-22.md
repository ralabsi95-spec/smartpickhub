## Comparison and editorial QA rules
1. **Do not rank air purifiers by claimed room coverage alone.** Normalize room area at the same ACH (prefer 4.8 ACH / 5 ACH) and compare CADR using the same unit. Some manufacturer pages show multiple room-size assumptions (4.8 ACH vs 2 ACH), and Blueair has updated DOE room-size data.
2. **Filter replacement is a recurring ownership cost.** Capture the exact compatible filter part number, price and stated replacement interval before any “best value” claim.
3. **Separate new, refurbished and discontinued products.** Philips AC1214/60 and AC2936/33 are discontinued; AC3033/30R1 is refurbished and not the same SKU as new AC3033/30.
4. **Do not conflate stock signals.** Add-to-cart on a manufacturer's own store, “Notify Me”, pre-order and retailer no-delivery signals are distinct. None proves Amazon availability.
5. **Heated airers differ in form factor and usable drying capacity.** A ladder towel rail, winged rack, mini tiered airer and 4-tier rack are not direct substitutes. Compare wattage, actual usable rail length, load, folded dimensions and cover inclusion.
6. **Running-cost estimates are tariff-dependent.** Do not present Lakeland/Dunelm “pence per hour” claims as universal.
