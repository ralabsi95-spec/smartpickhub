## Trend rationale
- UK warming products: Uswitch survey (6 Nov 2026) indicates nearly 11 million UK households plan to buy at least one warming gadget; 10% plan an electric blanket and 9% an electric heated throw. This is stated purchase intent, not search volume. https://www.uswitch.com/media-centre/2026/11/heat-child-o-mine-three-fifths-of-gen-z-households-plan-to-buy-warming-gadgets-as-energy-bills-set-to-rocket/
- UK household resilience: reporting after the Cabinet Office Be Ready campaign describes surges in torches, radios and power-bank purchases. Treat as a short-term campaign-linked signal and use calm everyday-emergency framing. https://www.aljazeera.com/features/2026/11/14/paranoid-or-practical-britains-be-ready-citizen
- US home environment: IHA 2026 State of the Industry summary reports intended purchases for air purifiers (41%), humidifiers (32%), water filters (31%), dehumidifiers (24%) and portable heaters (23%). Top purchase features are easy maintenance, safety and energy efficiency. https://www.theinspiredhomeshow.com/blog/staying-healthy-and-warm-this-winter
