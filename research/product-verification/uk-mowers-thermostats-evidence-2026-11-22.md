## Critical QA
1. Do not count Navimow bundles as separate mower models. Range pages list bundles as well as core models.
2. Do not treat the Navimow i210 LiDAR capacity as settled: current homepage and older indexed comparison differ. Hold the area claim pending SKU/manual.
3. The i210 LiDAR Pro is explicitly labelled EU version with UK adapter. Confirm adapter, warranty and package before treating it as a standard UK SKU.
4. Husqvarna's wire-free models require reliable connectivity and suitable installation conditions; these are not universally plug-and-play. Preserve Wi-Fi/satellite requirements in buyer guidance.
5. tado° X is not the same ecosystem as tado° V3+. Check boiler wiring, relay/OpenTherm, underfloor heating, hot-water control and Thread router needs before recommending.
6. Manufacturer claims are not independent test results. No hands-on testing is implied.
