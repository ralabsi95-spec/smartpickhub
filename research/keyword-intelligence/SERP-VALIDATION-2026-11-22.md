**Status:** Research-only. No page or product has been created, published or modified in production.

## Decision rules
- A visible commercial SERP confirms buying-guide intent, not a ranking opportunity or approval for a new URL.
- Strong independent-test competitors raise the differentiation and evidence bar. Never copy their rankings, scores, test claims or product conclusions.
- Keep regional intent separate where heating/HVAC systems, electrical standards, climate, terminology, models or availability materially differ.
- Consolidate room-size, pet, slope, capacity and compatibility modifiers unless search results demonstrate a distinct buyer task.
- This review adds no search-volume or trend metrics.
