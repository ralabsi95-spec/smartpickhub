## Interpretation and safeguards

- Monthly volume means provider-estimated average monthly searches, not site visits, clicks or sales.
- Buyer-intent score is a wording heuristic, not a conversion probability.
- Keyword variants can share grouped Google Ads volume. Do not sum variant volumes.
- Search trend history is not present in the cached 32-keyword list. A monthly trend must be collected from a fresh metrics export before claiming a rising/falling trend.
- Do not create one page per keyword. Cluster by SERP intent and map supporting terms to existing canonical guides first.
- No paid keyword research was started. Last-known OpenSEO balance was 28 credits; fresh metrics for 1,000 terms requires a separate costed research batch and approval.
- No live catalogue, guide, public URL, rank tracker or publication setting was changed.
