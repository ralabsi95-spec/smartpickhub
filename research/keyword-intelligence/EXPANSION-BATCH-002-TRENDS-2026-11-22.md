## Dated trend evidence
### UK — winter warming and household resilience
- Uswitch, updated 6 Nov 2026: nearly 11 million UK households plan to buy at least one warming gadget this winter. Survey responses report 10% planning an electric blanket and 9% an electric heated throw. This is stated purchase intent, not keyword volume. https://www.uswitch.com/media-centre/2026/11/heat-child-o-mine-three-fifths-of-gen-z-households-plan-to-buy-warming-gadgets-as-energy-bills-set-to-rocket/
- The Independent reports Maplin torch sales +284% and power-bank purchases +106% after the Cabinet Office Be Ready campaign. Al Jazeera (14 Nov 2026) reports wind-up radios, torches and power banks selling out at some retailers, with radio sales also rising at Robert Dyas. This is a campaign-linked retail spike, not proof of durable organic search volume. https://www.independent.co.uk/bulletin/news/uk-stockpile-be-ready-torch-batteries-b3072941.html and https://www.aljazeera.com/features/2026/11/14/paranoid-or-practical-britains-be-ready-citizen
- Official Cabinet Office advice concerns everyday emergencies such as severe weather and service disruption. Use a calm civil-resilience framing; do not use panic or war messaging. https://www.gov.uk/government/news/households-encouraged-to-take-small-simple-steps-to-stay-prepared-in-an-emergency
- Retail Gazette (3 Nov 2026) reports technology/electronics are planned purchases for 36.5% of surveyed UK Black Friday shoppers, while budgets and expected spend per shopper are more constrained. Emphasise value, warranty and ownership costs rather than unverified discount claims. https://www.retailgazette.co.uk/blog/2026/11/black-friday-discount/

### US — home-environment purchase intent and holiday discovery
- International Housewares Association's 2026 State of the Industry summary: intended 2026 purchases include air purifiers (41%), humidifiers (32%), water filters (31%), portable fans (27%), dehumidifiers (24%) and portable heaters (23%). Top feature considerations: easy maintenance (89%), safety (87%) and energy efficiency (84%). These are survey intentions, not search volume. https://www.theinspiredhomeshow.com/blog/staying-healthy-and-warm-this-winter
- Google Holiday 100 (9 Nov 2026) is curated from 2026 Search trends across tech, home and other categories. It confirms active holiday search-led discovery, but only specific queries have reported growth (e.g. podcast microphone +115%); it does not establish growth for every product category in our keyword file. https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/holiday-100-gift-guide-2026
- A US Black Friday category survey result surfaced electronics/technology as a major planned-purchase category, but the original CivicScience page redirected during verification. Retained as a low-confidence lead only, not a numeric metric. https://www.civicscience.com/black-friday-2026-in-store-shopping-gains-ground-as-early-deals-attract-interest
