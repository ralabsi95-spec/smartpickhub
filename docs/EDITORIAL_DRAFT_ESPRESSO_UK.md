## Working title and introduction

# Espresso Coffee Machines: Four Sage Options Compared for Different Home Setups

Choosing an espresso machine is partly about the coffee you want and partly about how much of the preparation process you want to do yourself. The four Sage machines below cover distinct workflows: a compact espresso machine that leaves grinding to you, two models with integrated grinders and manual milk texturing, and a more automated option with assisted grinding, tamping and milk preparation.

This guide focuses on the UK model families and manufacturer-documented features. Model suffixes, colour, included accessories, warranty and electrical ratings should still be checked against the exact retailer listing before ordering. The comparison is research-led; SmartPickHub has not conducted hands-on tests or independently measured coffee quality.

## Quick comparison

| Model | Workflow | Main point to consider |

## The four machines

### 1. Sage Bambino Plus — SES500 family

**Best suited to:** Someone who wants a relatively compact espresso machine and automatic milk texturing, and is comfortable selecting a separate grinder.

The Bambino Plus uses a 54mm portafilter and has a removable 1.9L water tank. Sage documents automatic milk texturing with three milk-temperature settings and three foam-level settings. The machine is supplied with single-wall and dual-wall filter baskets, supporting different approaches to espresso preparation.

**Potential advantages**
- Automatic milk texturing with selectable temperature and foam settings.
- 54mm portafilter and a 1.9L removable water tank.
- A relatively straightforward machine-only setup for buyers who prefer to choose their grinder separately.

**Points to consider**
- No integrated grinder is listed in the documented machine features; budget for a separate grinder if you need to grind whole beans.
- Automatic milk texturing does not remove the need to select suitable milk, clean the steam wand and maintain the machine.
- Check the exact SES500 suffix, included baskets and accessories on the retailer's listing.

**UK electrical rating in the official manual:** 220–240V, 50–60Hz, 1300–1600W.

### 2. Sage Barista Express — SES870 family

**Best suited to:** Someone who wants an integrated grinder and is willing to adjust the grind, dose and milk texture manually.

The Barista Express combines a conical burr grinder with the espresso machine. Sage documents 16 grind settings, a 54mm portafilter and an 18g dose reference. Its milk texturing is manual, and the product documentation describes PID temperature control at 93°C.

**Potential advantages**
- Integrated conical burr grinder with 16 grind settings.
- 54mm portafilter and a documented 18g dose reference.
- Manual milk texturing gives the user control over the steaming process.

**Points to consider**
- Integrated grinding reduces the need for a separate grinder, but it does not remove the learning involved in dialling in beans and adjusting extraction.
- Milk texturing is manual rather than an automatic milk programme.
- Allow time for cleaning the grinder area, portafilter and steam wand.

**UK electrical rating in the official manual:** 220–240V, 50–60Hz, 1560–1850W.

**Official evidence**
- Product page: https://www.sageappliances.com/en-gb/product/bes870
- UK instruction manual: https://assets.sageappliances.com/BES870/SES870_UK_SG2_A24_LR.pdf

### 3. Sage Barista Pro — SES878 family

**Best suited to:** Someone who wants an integrated grinder and a more digitally guided interface, but still prefers to texture milk manually.

The Barista Pro has an integrated conical burr grinder with 30 grind settings and Sage's ThermoJet heating system. Sage GB states a three-second heat-up time. The machine also has a digital interface and volumetric shot control. Milk microfoam is prepared manually, so it should not be confused with the Oracle Jet's automatic milk-texturing workflow.

**Potential advantages**
- Integrated grinder with 30 settings.
- ThermoJet heating system; Sage GB states a three-second heat-up.
- Digital interface and volumetric shot control.
- Manual milk texturing for users who want to control steaming themselves.

**Points to consider**
- The larger number of grind settings does not guarantee a better shot; bean freshness, grind choice, dose and technique still matter.
- Milk texturing remains a manual skill.
- Check the exact SES878 suffix and package contents before purchase.

**UK electrical rating in the official manual:** 220–240V, 50–60Hz, 1380–1650W.

**Official evidence**
- Product page: https://www.sageappliances.com/en-gb/product/bes878
- UK instruction manual: https://assets.sageappliances.com/BES878/SES878_UK_UG2_A25_LR.pdf

### 4. Sage Oracle Jet — SES985 family

**Best suited to:** Someone who values a more automated and guided preparation process, including assisted grinding, dosing, tamping and milk texturing.

The Oracle Jet is the most automation-focused machine in this comparison. Sage documents automatic grinding, dosing and tamping, a 58mm portafilter with a 22g dose, and 45 grind settings. Its Auto MilQ system provides automatic milk texturing with settings for dairy, soy, almond and oat milk. The machine also includes a touchscreen and Barista Guidance features.

**Potential advantages**
- Automatic grind, dose and tamp workflow.
- 58mm portafilter and a documented 22g dose.
- 45 grind settings.
- Auto MilQ milk texturing with dairy, soy, almond and oat settings.
- Touchscreen and Barista Guidance features.

**Points to consider**
- The more automated workflow may not suit someone who wants every espresso step to be fully manual.
- Check the dimensions, counter space, included accessories and exact SES985 suffix before purchase.
- Do not assume that every retailer bundle or regional variant includes identical accessories.

**UK electrical rating in the official manual:** 220–240V, 50–60Hz, 1400–1600W.

## What to check before buying

1. **Choose the workflow first.** Decide whether you want a separate grinder, an integrated grinder with manual adjustment, or a more automated grind-and-milk process.
2. **Confirm the exact UK model.** Match the SES model family and full suffix on the retailer listing to the manufacturer's UK documentation.
3. **Check electrical compatibility.** The ratings above are from UK manuals and must not be applied to US Breville variants or a listing with a different regional model.
4. **Compare the included accessories.** Check baskets, portafilter, milk jug, water filter, cleaning tools and any bundle-specific extras.
5. **Measure your counter space.** Compare the machine's current manufacturer dimensions with the available depth, height and clearance in your kitchen.
6. **Review care and cleaning.** Grinder cleaning, descaling, water-filter replacement and steam-wand cleaning are part of ownership.
7. **Check the retailer terms.** Confirm seller identity, new-versus-renewed condition, delivery to your UK postcode, returns and warranty before ordering.
